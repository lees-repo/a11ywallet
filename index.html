a11ywallet

    a11ywallet is a personal study companion. The notes paraphrase and explain the W3C Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 and the linked Understanding and Techniques documents, and the W3C ARIA Authoring Practices Guide (APG). The W3C documents are the normative source, so check them before you file a conformance decision.

    A reference collection for study. Nothing is tracked or scored.
